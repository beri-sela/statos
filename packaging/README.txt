STATOS - Staff and Tonic Sol-fa (offline copy)
==============================================

Statos is a music editor for tonic sol-fa and staff notation. This copy runs
entirely on your computer: no installation, no internet connection, no account.


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
1. Unzip this folder (right-click the zip file > "Extract All..." on Windows,
   or double-click it on a Mac).
2. Double-click  Statos.html
   It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

Tip: bookmark the page, or drag Statos.html to your desktop, so it is easy to
find next time.


WHERE YOUR SCORES ARE SAVED
---------------------------
- Your scores are saved automatically inside your web browser, on this computer.
  Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Keep Statos.html in the same folder and open it with the same browser each
  time, so the browser finds your scores again. (Some browsers, like Firefox,
  keep scores separately for each location of the file.)
- Clearing your browser's history / site data can delete saved scores.
  Use the "Save" button in Statos to keep a copy of each score as a file,
  and "Import" to open it again (in this copy, the online version, or on
  another computer).


UPDATING
--------
Download the newest zip from
  https://github.com/beri-sela/statos/releases
and replace Statos.html with the new one. Your saved scores stay in the browser
as long as the file keeps the same name and folder.


HELP
----
Click "Help" inside Statos for lessons, or press F1.
Questions and problem reports: https://github.com/beri-sela/statos/issues
